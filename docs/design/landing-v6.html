Questions before you start

Can any business use it?

Yes. HVAC, roofing, plumbing, dental, law and other trades ship as templates, not code paths. A custom industry template can be added for any other business.

What happens when I use my allowance?

Usage resets on each billing anniversary. Active subscribers can buy top-ups for conversations, voice minutes or emails.

Does the AI Manager use my conversations?

No. It is unmetered on paid plans, capped at 200 messages a day.

Can I change what the AI says?

Yes. Your prices, hours, service area and policies live in the AI Brain, and you can change them in plain language through the manager.

What happens when the minutes run out mid-call?

A call in progress is never cut off. New billable AI stops after a 24-hour grace period.

Where do the calls and recordings live?

Each business has its own workspace. Call recordings stream from the phone provider and are not stored by us.
