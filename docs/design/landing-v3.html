Questions, answered

No fine print gymnastics.

If the answer changes your decision, it belongs here.

Can I use Sagenify AI if I'm not a plumber, roofer or HVAC company?

Yes. The platform is generic and any business type can use it, including SaaS owners and agencies. The industry templates are starting points, not limits.

What happens when I run out of credits?

That AI service pauses gracefully when its allowance reaches zero. Paid plans can use top-ups, and an active voice call is never dropped mid-conversation.

Can I start without connecting every tool?

Yes. Start with the core workspace and add the integrations that fit your operation. Google Calendar is planned; Clerk, Flutterwave, Resend and Vapi are represented here as the connected stack.

Is Customer Finder included?

No. It is an optional add-on at $29 for 10 local prospects, presented separately so you stay in control of the choice.

Can an agency use Sagenify for its clients?

Yes. The platform is designed for isolated workspaces, making it suitable for agencies that manage more than one service business.
