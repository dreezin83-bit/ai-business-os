Questions

The things every owner asks first.

Do I have to change my phone number?
No. Forward your existing line to your Sagenify number, or set it to forward only after hours and at weekends. The number on the van stays the number on the van.
What happens when it doesn't know the answer?
It says so, takes a message, and hands the call to you by text and email within seconds. It never invents a price, a warranty or a promise on your behalf.
How long does setup actually take?
About thirty minutes. You enter your services, service area, hours and the questions you get asked every day. The knowledge base does the rest, and it sharpens with every real conversation.
What are credits, and will I run out?
Credits are AI usage. Every paid workspace includes 4,000 a month — roughly $20 of AI at our cost, which covers several hundred conversations including voice minutes. You get a warning at 15% remaining, then 24 hours of grace before anything is cut off. Top-ups start at $10.
Is my customer data separate from other businesses?
Yes. Every workspace is isolated — its own knowledge base, call records, leads and number. Nothing is shared between accounts and nothing is trained across them.
What is the founding member price lock?
$199 a month for as long as your subscription stays active — even after the standard $299 rate applies to new signups. If you cancel and come back later, you rejoin at the current rate.
